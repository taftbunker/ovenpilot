How the system works

  1. A table tent has a QR code: “Scan for a free appetizer.”
  2. The diner enters name, phone, and email on a mobile page.
  3. They're added to the restaurant's contact list (CRM).
  4. They instantly get a text + email with a claim code.
  5. The owner sends texts during slow hours (e.g. Monday 4 PM BOGO), birthdays, and weekly specials.
