Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. Questions: ovenpilothq@gmail.com or a message on Whop.

Privacy Terms Refunds & cancellation Subprocessors AI disclosure

The short version

  • This website has no cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and loads nothing from other companies.
  • For restaurants we work for, we handle their guests' details (like a name and phone number for a booking) only to run the service for that restaurant.
  • We never sell personal information, never share phone numbers for others' marketing, and don't use your data to train AI.
  • Marketing texts need a separate opt-in. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.
  • Email ovenpilothq@gmail.com to see or delete your information.

1. Who we are

Ovenpilot ("we", "us") is a small business in the Austin area, Texas, that sets up AI phone and text automations for local restaurants. This policy covers this website and the services we provide to restaurants. Contact: ovenpilothq@gmail.com. We don't publish a street address.

2. This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts or fonts. Since nothing tracks you, there is no cookie banner.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure; see GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive or use those logs.
  • The demo (the fictional Bella Cucina) saves what you type only in your own browser (local storage). It is never sent to us or anyone else, and no real texts or emails are sent. Use "Reset demo" or clear your browser data to remove it.
  • When you click "Book a call" you go to our store on Whop, which has its own privacy policy.

3. Restaurant clients (owners and staff)

When a restaurant hires us, we collect what we need to do the work: business and contact details (name, business email, phone), billing records (payments run through Whop; we never see full card numbers), and setup information such as menus, hours, policies, staff names and shift availability, and access to tools the restaurant chooses to connect. We use it to set up and run the service, bill for it, and talk to you about it. We keep business and billing records for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

4. Restaurant guests

When you call, text, book or order with a restaurant that uses Ovenpilot, we process your information on behalf of that restaurant. The restaurant decides how it is used, and we follow its instructions and our written agreement with it. Depending on what the restaurant uses, this can include:

  • your name and phone number, booking or order details, and any notes you give (for example an allergy or a birthday);
  • texts you send and receive with the restaurant, and when you opted in or out;
  • for phone calls answered by the AI receptionist: call details, a recording and transcript (the greeting tells you the call may be recorded), and a short summary;
  • a 1–5 score if you answer a review check-in;
  • an email address or birthday only if you choose to give one.

We collect only what each feature needs. We never ask for card numbers, passwords or Social Security numbers by phone or text.

5. Text messages (SMS terms)

  • Booking and order texts (confirmations, reminders, "your order is ready") go only to the number you gave for that booking or order.
  • Marketing texts (offers, slow-hour deals) need your separate opt-in, for example an unchecked box you tick on a table-QR form or texting a join keyword. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Restaurants send at most a few marketing texts a month, and never during quiet hours (9 PM–9 AM local).
  • Reply STOP to stop all texts from that restaurant, and HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except the service providers that deliver the messages.

6. How we use information, and what we never do

We use information only to provide, secure, support and improve the service for the restaurant it belongs to, and to meet legal duties. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for targeted advertising. We do not use client or guest data to train AI models, and where a provider gives us a choice, we choose settings that keep our data out of its model training. AI is used to answer calls and to draft wording from the restaurant's real data; see our AI disclosure.

7. Who we share it with

Only the service providers ("subprocessors") we need to run the service, such as our phone, texting, AI, hosting and email providers. They may use the data only to provide their service to us. The full list is on our subprocessors page. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or with a buyer if the business is ever sold (under this same policy).

9. Your choices and requests

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop marketing texts. Email ovenpilothq@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request" and the phone number or email it's about. We may confirm the request is really from you (for example by texting a code to that number). If we hold your data for a restaurant, we'll handle it with that restaurant. We aim to respond within 45 days. We won't treat you differently for making a request. If we delete your data but you had replied STOP, we keep your number on the do-not-text list only.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards: signed and secret-checked webhooks, access keys for admin tools, encrypted connections, rate limits, minimal access, and automatic deletion on the schedule above. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify the affected restaurant and people as the law requires.

11. Children

Ovenpilot is a service for businesses and isn't directed to children. Our services are not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll tell our restaurant clients before the change takes effect.
