The short version

These are the companies that may process personal information for us to run Ovenpilot. Not every client uses every provider; we connect only what your package needs. We'll update this page before adding or replacing a provider, and tell our clients.

ProviderWhat we use it forData involvedPolicy
Retell AIAI phone receptionist (voice calls)Caller phone number, call audio, transcripts, call summariesPrivacy
TwilioPhone numbers and text messages (SMS)Phone numbers, message content, opt-in/opt-out statusPrivacy
OpenAIDrafting wording (for example the morning brief) from the restaurant's dataShort summaries of bookings, orders and messagesPrivacy
Railway or RenderHosting the Ovenpilot automation server (one of the two)All service data stored on the serverPrivacy
Google (Calendar, Sheets, Gmail)Restaurant calendars and inventory or staff sheets; our business emailBooking details, staff schedules, emails with clientsPrivacy
ResendSending emails (morning brief, supplier orders)Recipient email addresses and email contentPrivacy
WhopOur store, checkout, payments and client messagesClient names, emails, billing and payment detailsPrivacy
GitHub PagesHosting this website (no client or guest data)Visitor IP addresses in GitHub's own security logsPrivacy

Render's privacy policy is at render.com/privacy. If a restaurant asks us to connect its own tools (for example a Square POS), those companies act for the restaurant under its own agreements with them.

We don't use advertising, analytics or data-broker services. We may switch our voice provider to Vapi or add a managed database (Supabase or Neon) in the future. If we do, we'll update this list first.
