The short version

  • Our phone receptionist is an AI, and it says so at the start of every call. It also tells callers the call may be recorded.
  • Texts are automated. Replies we don't recognize go to a person at the restaurant, not to an AI.
  • AI writes wording from the restaurant's real data; it doesn't invent prices, hours or availability.
  • We don't use client or guest data to train AI models.

The AI phone receptionist

Restaurants on Ovenpilot can have calls answered by an AI voice assistant. It opens every call with a line like: "Thanks for calling [restaurant]. This is [name], the restaurant's AI assistant, and this call may be recorded." If a caller asks whether they're talking to a person, it says no. It can take bookings and to-go orders and answer questions from the facts the restaurant gave us. It transfers to staff (or takes a message) when a caller asks for a person, has a complaint, or needs something it can't help with. It never asks for card numbers, passwords or Social Security numbers. For serious allergies, it asks callers to confirm with staff.

Automated texts and emails

Booking confirmations, reminders, review check-ins and offers are sent automatically using templates the restaurant approved. Some wording, like the owner's morning brief, is drafted by an AI model from the restaurant's real numbers; the numbers themselves come from the data, not the AI. Inbound texts that aren't simple replies (like YES or STOP) are forwarded to a person at the restaurant instead of being answered by AI.

Limits

AI can mishear, misunderstand or make mistakes. Restaurants review the information the AI uses, and staff can always take over. If something an AI told you seems wrong, please call the restaurant and speak with a person.

Your data and AI training

We don't use client or guest data to train AI models, and where our providers offer a choice, we choose settings that keep our data out of their training. See our Privacy Policy and subprocessors.

This website

Some of this website's text and graphics were drafted with help from AI tools and reviewed by Ovenpilot. There are no photos of real people, customer logos or testimonials on this site. "Bella Cucina", its menu, guests and phone numbers in the demo are fictional, and the demo's texts and dashboard are simulated.
