Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. Questions: ovenpilothq@gmail.com or a message on Whop.

Privacy Terms Refunds & cancellation Subprocessors AI disclosure

The short version

  • Cancel any time by email or a Whop message. No reason needed, no cancellation fee, no call required.
  • Monthly plans renew automatically until you cancel. After you give notice, at most one more month is billed (the 30-day notice period), and then billing stops.
  • The setup fee is fully refunded if you cancel before work starts. Once work has started it isn't refundable, and unpaid setup installments are still due.
  • Charged twice or by mistake? We refund it in full.

How to cancel

Email ovenpilothq@gmail.com or send us a message on Whop saying you want to cancel. That's it. We'll confirm in writing, with the date your service and billing end. If you pay through Whop, you can also cancel the monthly subscription from your Whop account; please tell us too, so we can wind down your automations properly.

Monthly fees and auto-renewal

  • Monthly plans renew automatically every month until cancelled.
  • Cancellation takes effect 30 days after you tell us. That notice period is billed, and nothing after it.
  • A month that has already started isn't refunded. Months that haven't started are never charged.

Setup fees

  • Before work starts (before the kickoff call and setup): full refund.
  • After work has started: the setup fee isn't refundable, because the work has been done or is underway.
  • Installment plans: if you cancel while paying the setup in installments, the remaining installments are still due.

Billing mistakes

If we charge you twice, charge the wrong amount, or charge you after your cancellation took effect, tell us and we'll refund the difference in full as soon as we confirm it.

What happens after you cancel

We switch off your automations at the end date, hand back your phone-number forwarding and accounts, give you an export of your data on request, and delete your restaurant's guest data within 30 days, as described in our Privacy Policy.
